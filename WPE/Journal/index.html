What is WPE?

WPE stands for Whole-Person Education. It is a General Education course focused on developing students holistically, rather than only academically. Silliman's curriculum lists GE 10 as "Whole Person Education (WPE)."

The idea of WPE is that a university education should develop the whole person intellectually, spiritually, ethically, socially, and personally. It prepares students for a career and encourages them to become people who can learn, live, serve, and contribute meaningfully to society.

A major part of GE 10 is understanding Silliman University history, institutional heritage, Christian identity, vision, mission, and traditions.Students learn about how Silliman began as Silliman Institute on August 28, 1901, and how it developed into a university. The course also examines important events and traditions that shaped the Sillimanian community.

GE 10 discusses Silliman's 5 C's, which describe important areas where the university engages students and society:

  • Classroom
  • Church
  • Court
  • Culture
  • Community
This means that education doesn't happen only inside a classroom. Students encounter learning through faith, culture, civic life, and engagement with the community.
