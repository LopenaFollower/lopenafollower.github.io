The 5C's

The 5C's highlight the 5 areas where the student can develop to become complete, accomplished, and well versed, rather than just learning the concepts inside the classroom and focusing on academics.

  1. Classroom - Intellectual Development

    The Classroom is where the mind develops. Students gain knowledge, learn to think critically, and develop the skills needed to understand the world around them in this area.

  2. Church - Spiritual and Moral

    The Church represents the development of the spiritual and moral side of a person. It encourages students to reflect on their beliefs, values, purpose, and relationship with others.

  3. Court - Physical Development

    The Court represents the development of the physical aspect of the student. Sports and other physical activities teach students that taking care of the body is an important part of becoming a healthy and disciplined individual.

  4. Culture - Creative and Cultural Development

    Culture represents the students' ability to appreciate, understand, and participate in the creativity and cultural expression of humans. Culture helps students understand that people have different ways of seeing and expressing the world. By experiencing different forms of culture, we can become more open-minded and appreciative of diversity, and to also appreciate deeply our own.

  5. Community - Social Development

    The Community represents our relationship with other people and society. Education shouldn't only prepare someone to succeed individually. It should also help them understand that they are part of a larger community and therefore have responsibilities toward others.
